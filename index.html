About the wall

A living gallery of moments

Moments is an interactive photography wall built to feel less like a grid and more like a room you can move through. The mosaic drifts and parts wherever your cursor travels, opening a quiet space around whatever catches your eye.

Click any frame and it glides to the centre, where the full image, its place, and its year come into focus. Nothing is fixed in place, so every visit reveals the collection in a slightly different arrangement.

The project is a small experiment in giving a photo archive a sense of motion and depth, turning browsing into something closer to wandering.
